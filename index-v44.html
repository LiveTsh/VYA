HOW VYA WORKS

Shared: a seat in a shared car for R23. You pay the driver cash when you arrive.

Express: a direct ride. The price is shown before you confirm and it never goes up because it is busy.

Cash: VYA never takes your ride money. You pay the driver.

Drivers: keep the whole fare and pay a flat access fee, not a cut of each trip.

BEFORE YOU GET IN

Check that the car’s make, colour and registration match what VYA shows you.

Tell someone you trust where you are going and the car’s registration.

If you do not feel safe, you can end the ride with “I don’t feel safe” on the ride screen.

Never pay more than the price shown. If a driver asks for more, say no and report it.
